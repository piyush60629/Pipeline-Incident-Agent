# INC-2041: Customer ingestion failed after secret expiry

- Date: 2026-04-02
- Pipeline: pl_ingest_customers
- Symptom: Copy activity failed with 'Server failed to authenticate the request' on the CRM landing storage account.
- Root cause: The client secret of service principal sp-crm-ingest had expired; nobody was alerted.
- Resolution: New secret created, Key Vault secret updated, connection tested, pipeline rerun after approval.
- Prevention: Requested a Key Vault expiry alert (still pending at the time of closing).
