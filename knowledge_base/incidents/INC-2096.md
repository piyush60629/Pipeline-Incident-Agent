# INC-2096: Product master copy failed with connection reset

- Date: 2026-10-14
- Pipeline: pl_ingest_products
- Symptom: SqlFailedToConnect, pre-login handshake error, "connection was forcibly closed by the remote host".
- Root cause: Short TCP resets on the ExpressRoute gateway during the network maintenance window (02:20-02:35).
- Resolution: Manual rerun at 02:55 succeeded without changes.
- Prevention: Network team asked to move maintenance outside the 02:00-03:00 load window.
