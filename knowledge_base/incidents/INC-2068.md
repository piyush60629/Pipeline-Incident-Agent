# INC-2068: Gold sales fact timed out during sale season

- Date: 2026-07-14
- Pipeline: pl_transform_gold_sales
- Symptom: Activity timed out after 60 minutes; next day reprocessed the same data because the watermark was not updated.
- Root cause: Sales volume grew during a sale; the fact table is rewritten in full every run.
- Resolution: Timeout raised to 90 minutes and the batch rerun after approval.
- Prevention: Proposed partitioning gold.fact_sales by transaction_date and switching to incremental MERGE (backlog item DE-311).
